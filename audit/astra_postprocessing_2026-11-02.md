Implement **automatic roster assignment on short track pieces, with bounded evidence and an explicit “unknown” option**. Refine the planned offline identity stage so that it can assign different identities to different portions of one UCMC track. That is the operation most directly justified by the measured failures; my expectation that it offers the best improvement per engineering hour is a project-specific hypothesis, not an established result.

Keep the saved current UCMC configuration fixed: `cdt=8`, ambiguity margin 1, measurement-noise scaling 3, and no emitted coast rows. Its improved box AssA and IDF1 coexist with purity falling from .978 to .865 and modal-owner disagreements increasing from 1,369 to 8,467. Those disagreements are not literal swap counts, but they demonstrate that treating every anonymous track as one person is unsafe. Whole-track majority voting could confidently spread an identity across substantial wrong-person sections. Conversely, merely reconnecting anonymous fragments earns no independent TI-HOTA reward once their exact roster identities are already correct.

The proposed decoder would work as follows:

- Divide each anonymous track at detection gaps and player-overlap intervals, and impose a short maximum piece duration so that an undetected within-team switch cannot contaminate an entire track. Select that duration on official training data. Retain every piece’s original detection rows and court coordinates.
- Score each piece against the six game-specific team–jersey identities. Combine available colour evidence with jersey probabilities, weighted by digit readability for the jersey term only. Use temporally spaced observations and cap accumulated log-evidence; hundreds of adjacent crops must not count as hundreds of independent confirmations.
- Choose piece labels jointly across the video, allowing “unknown.” Enforce at most one emitted observation per roster identity per frame, with no requirement to fill all six roster slots. Add a bounded continuity preference only between plausible, unambiguous neighbouring pieces; allow contradictory reliable evidence to override it.
- Omit unknown pieces from the submission and preserve their records internally. Let identities recur across disconnected pieces without requiring anonymous track merging.

This is a small constrained assignment problem over pieces and six labels, implementable with a standard integer optimizer. The optimizer is an additional dependency to verify, not a component known to exist locally. Crucially, continuity must remain weaker than reliable identity evidence: otherwise the decoder would recreate UCMC’s mixed-track problem offline.

SportsSUSHI supplies relevant primary evidence: its authors report improved sports association from combining jersey numbers, team identities, and field coordinates in a hierarchical tracker. That supports using complementary evidence over time; it does **not** validate this simpler decoder or establish a gain on TrackID3x3. Its official repository currently says the code is forthcoming, strengthening the case against adopting that complete system before this deadline. [SportsSUSHI paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21242), [official repository](https://github.com/mkoshkina/sports-SUSHI).

The strongest alternative is conservative gap interpolation with bidirectional smoothing. It is attractive because the current output suppresses coast rows, and StrongSORT reports benefits from Gaussian-smoothed interpolation alongside its association improvements on standard MOT benchmarks. Those results establish plausibility, not transfer to this identity-conditioned court metric. [StrongSORT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13514). Here, smoothing has no demonstrated local advantage over raw positions, does not fix absent or wrong roster identities, and can manufacture confidently wrong rows across a switch. Localization also follows a bounding-box projection convention in training, so making trajectories look physically smoother need not improve their measured accuracy. Identity repair therefore has the stronger current causal case.

Necessary submission plumbing remains separate from that marginal benefit. A detector-box torso-crop path, stable detection-to-score lineage, inference with the fine-tuned models, roster mapping, and a validated writer are prerequisites for any meaningful identity-labelled baseline. None is made unnecessary by post-processing. Digit readability is not player eligibility, and a missing colour prediction must mean unavailable evidence, not “non-player.” The current nine-number predictor can supply jersey scores; the four-colour-plus-none output is not yet established. Fine-tuning underway is valuable, but completed deployment accuracy remains unknown.
